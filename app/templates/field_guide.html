A practical field guide for Esan farmers

A concise reference for planning a crop, caring for soil and water, managing common farm problems responsibly and protecting people and the environment.

1. Plan before planting

  • Choose crops suited to your land, local conditions and available markets.
  • Use healthy, suitable seed and plan for land preparation, labour, water and storage.
  • Keep a simple farm record of planting dates, inputs, field observations, harvest and costs.

2. Care for soil and water

  • Protect soil cover where practical and avoid leaving soil bare for long periods.
  • Rotate crops and use organic matter to support soil structure and fertility.
  • Reduce erosion by managing runoff and keeping drains and waterways clear of waste.
  • Use water efficiently and keep fertilizers and pesticides away from wells, streams and ponds.

3. Monitor crops and respond early

  • Walk through fields regularly and check both healthy and affected plants.
  • Identify the problem before choosing a response; symptoms can have different causes.
  • Use prevention, good field hygiene and appropriate crop practices as part of integrated pest management.
  • Ask a qualified agricultural adviser when the cause or best response is unclear.

4. Use agricultural chemicals responsibly

  • Use only a registered product suitable for the crop and problem, and follow its label exactly.
  • Wear the protective equipment stated on the label. Keep children, animals, food and water away during handling and application.
  • Never guess a dose, mix products unless the label allows it, or reuse chemical containers.
  • Store products securely in their original labelled containers and dispose of containers as directed by the label and local guidance.
  • Observe all re-entry and pre-harvest intervals on the label.

5. Protect the farm environment

  • Prevent chemical spills and runoff, and keep farm waste out of waterways.
  • Maintain buffer areas around sensitive places and follow product-label restrictions.
  • Choose practices that conserve soil, support biodiversity and reduce unnecessary input use.
  • Report serious spills or suspected exposure promptly and follow local emergency guidance.
Important: This guide is general educational information, not a substitute for product labels, local regulations or advice from a qualified agricultural professional.
