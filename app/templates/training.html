SAFE CHEMICAL USE

Four steps to safer handling

Always follow the product label and local guidance. Never guess a dose or mix products unless the label specifically allows it.

01 / CHECK

Read the label first

Confirm the product is registered for the crop and problem. Ask a qualified adviser if anything is unclear.

02 / PREPARE

Wear the required protection

Use every item of protective equipment listed on the label. Keep children, animals, food, and water away from the work area.

03 / APPLY

Follow label directions

Use the product only as directed. Do not guess doses or mix products unless the label specifically allows it.

04 / STORE SAFELY

Clean up and store securely

Keep products sealed in their original labelled containers, locked away from children, food, feed and water. Follow the label for storage and disposal.
